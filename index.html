Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OEMMart and Ollie

How does Ollie's AI matching algorithm work?

Ollie uses advanced machine learning to analyze government tender documents and compare them against your business profile, capabilities, and past performance. It evaluates thousands of data points including requirements, certifications, contract size, location, and historical success rates to generate a relevance score. The algorithm continuously learns from outcomes to improve matching accuracy over time.

What types of government contracts does OEMMart cover?

OEMMart aggregates opportunities from federal, state, and local government agencies across all industries. This includes construction, IT services, professional services, equipment and supplies, facilities management, and more. We monitor major procurement portals like SAM.gov, state procurement sites, and municipal contracting systems to ensure comprehensive coverage.

Can OEMMart really help me draft proposals automatically?

Yes! Our AI drafting feature analyzes the tender requirements and generates a customized proposal outline including executive summary, technical approach, past performance examples, and pricing structure. While you'll want to review and refine the content, it dramatically reduces the time spent on initial draft creation from days to hours. The AI learns from your approved proposals to better match your writing style and approach.

Is there a free trial available?

Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all Professional plan features. No credit card required to start. This gives you unlimited tender matching, Ollie analysis, and up to 10 AI-drafted proposals. You can cancel anytime during the trial period with no obligations.

How quickly will I start seeing matched opportunities?

Most users see their first matched opportunities within 24 hours of completing their business profile. The system immediately begins scanning active tenders and analyzing fit scores. You'll receive daily email notifications for high-scoring matches, and our real-time dashboard updates continuously as new opportunities are posted.

We integrate with popular CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot), project management tools (Monday.com, Asana), and document management platforms (Google Drive, Dropbox, SharePoint). Professional and Enterprise plans also support custom API integrations and webhooks for seamless workflow automation with your existing systems.
