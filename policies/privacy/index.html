Overview

OEMMart Inc. (“OEMMart,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit www.oemmart.com or use our services, including our AI-powered procurement platform, the Ollie AI assistant, and SignalForge market intelligence service (collectively, the “Services”).

This Policy applies to website visitors, registered users, and customers. If a separate written agreement with OEMMart addresses personal information or the Services, that agreement also applies. If it conflicts with this Policy, the written agreement governs to the extent of the conflict.

This Policy is intended as a general privacy notice and is not legal advice. OEMMart recommends professional legal review before publication and periodically thereafter.

01Who We Are

OEMMart Inc. is incorporated under the laws of Ontario, Canada, and operates the website and Services described in this Policy.

For privacy questions or requests, contact:

02Scope and Applicable Privacy Laws

OEMMart is based in Canada and handles personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

Where applicable, this Policy also describes privacy rights for individuals in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California. The rights available to you may depend on where you live and which laws apply to OEMMart’s processing of your information.

Where practical, OEMMart seeks to apply a high level of privacy protection to all users, regardless of location.

03Personal Information We Collect

“Personal information” generally means information about an identifiable individual. The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

3.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Contact details, such as your name, business email address, telephone number, company, and job title.
  • Account information, such as login credentials, account settings, and subscription details.
  • Business profile information you provide, such as your products, capabilities, certifications, industries, and target markets. This information helps us match your profile with tenders or market signals.
  • Content you upload or submit to the Services, such as tender documents, RFPs, RFQs, proposals, business documents, and prompts or questions submitted to Ollie.
  • Information you provide when contacting us, requesting support, responding to surveys, or communicating with us.

Please do not submit personal, sensitive, confidential, or regulated information unless you are authorized to do so and its submission is appropriate for the Services.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or use the Services, we may automatically collect technical and usage information, such as:

  • IP address and general location inferred from it.
  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and language settings.
  • Pages or platform features accessed, referring pages, and the date, time, and duration of visits.
  • Interactions with website or platform features, and diagnostic, security, and log information.

3.3 Information from Other Sources

We may receive information from business contacts, service providers, publicly available sources, or organizations that administer or refer you to our Services. We may combine such information with information you provide, where permitted by law.

04How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, maintaining, and supporting the website and Services.
  • Matching business profiles with government tenders or market signals.
  • Supporting AI-assisted proposal drafting, contract analysis, document search, market intelligence feeds, and notifications.
  • Creating, administering, and securing accounts, and managing subscriptions.
  • Personalizing matching and other features based on the business profile and settings you provide.
  • Communicating about accounts, service delivery, support, changes, and security matters.
  • Sending newsletters, product updates, and other marketing communications where we have obtained consent or are otherwise permitted to do so by law.
  • Understanding usage, measuring performance, and improving the website and Services.
  • Detecting, preventing, and investigating misuse, fraud, security incidents, or other harmful activity.
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting our rights, users, and business.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing does not stop service-related or other transactional communications.

05Customer Content and AI

Customers retain ownership of content and materials they provide to OEMMart, including business profiles, tender documents, and uploaded materials (“Customer Content”). OEMMart uses Customer Content to provide the Services to the relevant customer, including to generate matches, analyses, drafts, search results, and other requested outputs.

OEMMart does not use Customer Content to train or improve AI models for the benefit of other customers.

As between OEMMart and the customer, information derived from Customer Content and content generated within the platform, including outputs, drafts, analyses, match results, and other platform-generated content, is owned by OEMMart to the extent set out in the applicable service agreement or terms. OEMMart may use such derived or generated content to operate, maintain, and improve the Services, subject to applicable confidentiality obligations and de-identification or aggregation where appropriate.

This section does not change any rights individuals have under applicable privacy laws. Customer Content may contain personal information about individuals other than the account holder. Customers are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary authority to provide that information to OEMMart.

06Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses essential cookies and analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies for targeted advertising.

  • Essential cookies support core website functions, security, and account sessions. These are necessary for the website or Services to operate.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand website and feature usage, measure performance, and improve our services. Analytics may be provided through tools similar to Google Analytics.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect website or account functionality. Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential analytics cookies. Browser settings may not remove cookies already stored on your device, so you may need to delete them through your browser.

07How We Disclose Personal Information

OEMMart does not sell personal information. We disclose personal information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to:

  • Service providers and processors that support cloud hosting, AI infrastructure, email delivery, payment processing, analytics, security, and other business operations. They are required to protect information and use it only for authorized purposes.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers, where necessary for their services.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or where necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Parties involved in a corporate transaction, such as a merger, financing, reorganization, or acquisition. Where required, we will provide notice and ensure appropriate protections for personal information.

Payments are processed by third-party payment processors. OEMMart does not store full payment card numbers. Payment processors handle payment information under their own terms and privacy practices.

08International Data Transfers

Personal information may be stored or processed in data centers in Canada and the United States. This means information collected in Canada may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from the United States.

We use service providers, including cloud hosting, AI processing, email, analytics, and payment providers, under contractual or other safeguards intended to protect personal information and limit its use to authorized purposes. Where information is processed outside your home jurisdiction, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement, or government authorities under applicable law.

For individuals in the European Economic Area, the European Commission has recognized Canada as providing adequate protection for certain transfers to Canadian commercial organizations subject to PIPEDA. This recognition does not apply to every recipient or circumstance. Where an adequacy decision does not apply, transfers may rely on appropriate safeguards permitted by applicable data protection law.

09Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, meet contractual and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate business records.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, how it is used, and applicable legal requirements. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete it or de-identify it. Copies may remain in routine backups for a limited period before they are deleted or overwritten.

10Security

OEMMart uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification. These measures may include encryption in transit, access controls, and limiting access to personnel and service providers who need the information for authorized purposes.

No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly at privacy@oemmart.com.

If a security incident creates a real risk of significant harm or triggers other notification obligations, we will notify affected individuals and regulators as required by applicable law.

11Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent, or request deletion or other restrictions on its use.

To make a request, email privacy@oemmart.com. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to verify your identity and locate relevant records. We may request additional information to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfill a request where an exception or legal obligation applies.

We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Under PIPEDA, organizations generally must respond to access requests within 30 days, subject to permitted extensions and exceptions.

You may also raise a concern or complaint with us using the contact details in this Policy. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the relevant provincial privacy regulator.

12Additional Rights for EEA and UK Residents

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) or UK GDPR applies, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format or request its transfer to another organization.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests and, at any time, object to direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Where required, we process personal information on the basis of consent, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as operating, securing, and improving our Services. You may contact us at privacy@oemmart.com to exercise applicable rights or ask about the basis for a particular processing activity.

As described in Section 8, personal information may be processed in Canada and the United States. The European Commission’s adequacy recognition for Canada is limited to certain transfers to Canadian commercial organizations subject to PIPEDA. Where it does not apply, we use appropriate safeguards where required by law.

13Additional Rights for California Residents

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CCPA/CPRA”), applies to OEMMart and your information, you may have rights to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, or otherwise process.
  • Request access to or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined by California law.
  • Receive equal service and pricing without discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

OEMMart does not sell personal information. OEMMart does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly collect or process sensitive personal information for purposes that require offering a limitation right under California law.

To make a request, email privacy@oemmart.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and authority to make the request.

14Children’s Privacy

The website and Services are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 16 or individuals below the applicable age of majority. OEMMart does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact privacy@oemmart.com so we can review and take appropriate steps.

15Third-Party Websites and Services

The website or Services may link to third-party websites or services. This Policy does not apply to those third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing information.

16Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on our website and change the Last Updated date above. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice, such as by email to registered users where required by law. Your continued use of the website or Services after an updated Policy takes effect is subject to the revised Policy, to the extent permitted by law.
